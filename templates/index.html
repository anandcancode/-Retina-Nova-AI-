Clinical Background

What is Diabetic Retinopathy?

Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is a progressive neurovascular complication of diabetes mellitus that damages the blood vessels supplying the light-sensitive retina at the back of the eye.

Chronically elevated blood sugar leads to capillary occlusion, blood-retina barrier breakdown, leakage of fluid and blood (microaneurysms, hemorrhages, and exudates), and eventually pathological neovascularization. If left undiagnosed, it causes irreversible blindness.

Intelligent Workflow

How the AI Detection System Works

From raw fundus photograph to multi-class diagnostic grade in four streamlined stages.

1. Image Ingestion

Upload standard digital fundus photographs (JPG/PNG). The system verifies resolution, color distribution, and retinal boundary integrity.

2. Ben Graham Preprocessing

Applies circular aperture masking, crops uninformative dark margins, and computes Gaussian local contrast subtraction to amplify microaneurysms and exudates.

3. Hybrid Feature Fusion

Dual neural branches extract both local micro-lesion textures (Custom CNN) and high-level global context (EfficientNet), fused into a unified latent feature space.

4. Softmax Classification

Projects the fused vectors through dense regularized layers to yield class-wise probabilities across all 5 ICDR Diabetic Retinopathy stages.

5. Clinical Recommendations

Generates stage-specific ophthalmic follow-up intervals, urgency flags, and suggested diagnostic interventions (OCT, Fluorescein Angiography).

6. Formal PDF Report

One-click export of an official medical report featuring side-by-side original and enhanced scans, probability charts, and diagnostic sign-off blocks.
